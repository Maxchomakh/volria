Three pillars, one chain

Volria studies how three ideas can work together in an EVM-compatible network. Each one is tested separately before being combined.

DAG

Blocks that can be produced in parallel and merge together, instead of waiting in a single line.

Proof-of-Work

Each block is secured by computation, a model proven by over a decade of public blockchains.

Encryption

Privacy studied from the design stage, as a research track for future versions.
